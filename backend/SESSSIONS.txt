# Shift A → Shift B Logic in Kitchen Sessions

This logic applies **only to TWO_SHIFT branches**.

Current pilot example:

```text id="uul7km"
Bomet Town branch_id=2 = TWO_SHIFT
Mogogosiek branch_id=5 = SINGLE_SHIFT
```

So:

```text id="idjv3g"
Bomet = Shift A → Shift B flow
Mogogosiek = no Shift A/B, only one daily session
```

The production setup already has `branch_shift_config`, `kitchen_shifts`, `kitchen_shift_items`, and `kitchen_shift_stock_take` for this Kitchen Sessions structure. 

---

# 1. Main idea

Shift A and Shift B are **two kitchen sub-shifts inside one restaurant business day**.

```text id="z3whbq"
Restaurant cashier shift / business day
        ↓
Kitchen Shift A
        ↓ handover stocktake
Kitchen Shift B
```

Shift A does not just close blindly. The storekeeper must count what is left, then that count becomes Shift B opening stock.

---

# 2. Full flow

```text id="uurqlh"
Morning:
Opening Kitchen Stocktake
→ Shift A opens

During Shift A:
Stock issued
Production logged
Spoilage recorded
POS/event usage happens

End of Shift A:
Storekeeper performs handover stocktake
→ System calculates Shift A variance
→ Shift A closes
→ Remaining physical stock becomes Shift B opening stock

Shift B:
Continues with the remaining kitchen stock
Additional stock can be issued if needed
Production can be logged if needed
Stocktake/variance continues

End of business day:
Shift B closes with the restaurant cashier day
Next day opening stocktake confirms final variance
```

---

# 3. What happens at Shift A close?

At the end of Shift A, storekeeper enters physical count.

Example:

```text id="rb53rr"
Shift A opening potatoes: 20kg
Stock added during A: 30kg
Expected balance: 15kg
Physical count at handover: 13kg
```

System calculates:

```text id="3gixm9"
Variance = Physical Count - Expected Balance
Variance = 13kg - 15kg
Variance = -2kg shortage
```

Then:

```text id="fuh4rx"
Shift A closes with 13kg physical potatoes.
Shift B opens with 13kg potatoes.
```

So the rule is:

```text id="zijp0g"
Shift A physical handover count = Shift B opening stock
```

---

# 4. What Shift B receives

Shift B should receive:

```text id="xth3fk"
- all remaining raw stock
- all remaining produced finished stock
- all unresolved items carried forward
- opening stock equal to Shift A physical handover count
```

Example:

```text id="8ak6jf"
Shift A handover count:
Potatoes: 13kg
Beef: 4kg
Mango juice: 8 litres

Shift B opening:
Potatoes: 13kg
Beef: 4kg
Mango juice: 8 litres
```

---

# 5. Important validations

The system must block wrong flow.

```text id="do82be"
1. Shift B cannot open before Shift A is closed.
2. Shift A cannot close without handover stocktake.
3. Shift B opening stock must come from Shift A physical count.
4. Storekeeper cannot manually invent Shift B opening stock.
5. Accountant approval should not block Shift B opening.
6. Large variance needs explanation/recount.
7. Extreme variance needs supervisor override before handover continues.
```

# 8. Simple backend algorithm

```text id="ivhmge"
IF branch shift_mode != TWO_SHIFT:
    Do not show Shift A/B
    Use single daily session only

IF branch shift_mode == TWO_SHIFT:
    Open Shift A after opening kitchen stocktake

When closing Shift A:
    Require physical handover stocktake
    Calculate variance
    Require explanation if large variance
    Require supervisor override if extreme variance
    Save Shift A physical counts
    Mark Shift A closed
    Create/open Shift B
    Set Shift B opening_stock = Shift A physical_count

When Shift B closes:
    Close with business day
    Next day opening stocktake reveals final variance
```

# Final rule

```text id="wjtpwy"
Shift A does not pass stock to Shift B by assumption.

Shift A must physically count stock at handover.

That physical count becomes Shift B opening stock.

Any difference between expected and physical count becomes Shift A variance for accountant review.
```
Below is the recommended **Shift A → Shift B Kitchen Sessions logic** for **Bomet Town**, which uses `TWO_SHIFT` mode.

# KITCHEN SHIFT A TO SHIFT B HANDOVER LOGIC

## 1. Shift A Opening

The Opening Kitchen Stocktake starts **Shift A** for the business date.

Shift A opening stock may come from:

* The previous day’s Shift B closing stock.
* A manually entered opening stocktake when there is no previous closing stock.
* New issues received from the Branch Main Store after opening.

The opening stocktake is only an opening balance. It must **not deduct stock from the Branch Main Store**.

---

## 2. Shift A Operations

All kitchen transactions performed while Shift A is open must be attached to:

* Branch
* Business date
* Kitchen session
* Shift A
* Responsible employee
* Transaction timestamp

This includes:

* Main Store issues
* Returns to Main Store
* POS recipe consumption
* Conference consumption
* Buffet consumption
* Accommodation breakfast consumption
* Outside catering consumption
* Staff meals
* Production raw-material usage
* Production finished output
* Transfers
* Wastage
* Manual stock adjustments

Transactions must never be posted to Shift A after its handover has been completed.

---

## 3. Starting the Shift A Handover

The storekeeper selects:

**Close Shift A / Handover to Shift B**

The system changes Shift A to:

`HANDOVER_IN_PROGRESS`

During the handover:

* New Shift A transactions should be blocked.
* The storekeeper performs a physical count of every kitchen stock item.
* All required production, consumption, wastage, issue and return records must be submitted.
* Any unsubmitted wastage must block completion of the handover.
* Accountant approval of submitted wastage does not have to block the handover.

---

## 4. Expected Shift A Closing Stock

For every kitchen item, the system calculates:

**Expected Closing Stock**

`Opening Stock`
`+ Receipts and Transfers In`
`+ Production Output`
`- POS Recipe Consumption`
`- Other Consumption Channels`
`- Production Raw Materials Used`
`- Returns and Transfers Out`
`- Wastage`
`± Approved Adjustments`

The storekeeper enters the physical closing quantity.

The system calculates:

`Variance = Physical Closing Stock - Expected Closing Stock`

A negative figure represents a shortage.

A positive figure represents an excess.

Where a variance exceeds the configured tolerance, the storekeeper must enter:

* Variance reason
* Explanation
* Responsible employee or shift team, where known
* Supporting comments

---

## 5. Closing Shift A

Shift A can only close when:

* The physical handover count is complete.
* All mandatory items have been counted.
* Required Shift A records have been submitted.
* All wastage has been recorded and submitted.
* Mandatory variance explanations have been entered.
* There is no existing open Shift B.

After submission, Shift A changes to:

`CLOSED_PENDING_ACCOUNTANT_REVIEW`

Shift B does **not** need to wait for the accountant to approve Shift A.

---

## 6. Opening Shift B

Shift B can only be created after Shift A reaches:

`CLOSED_PENDING_ACCOUNTANT_REVIEW`

Shift B must use the same:

* Branch
* Business date
* Kitchen session

The system automatically copies:

`Shift A Physical Closing Stock → Shift B Opening Stock`

This is a stock carry-forward, not a new Main Store issue.

The system must therefore:

* Create the Shift B opening stock snapshot.
* Create a handover audit record.
* Link Shift B to Shift A.
* Avoid deducting any quantities from the Branch Main Store.
* Avoid creating duplicate stock receipts.

Shift B opening stock should normally be read-only because it came from the completed Shift A handover.

After confirmation, Shift B changes to:

`OPEN`

---

## 7. Handover Audit Record

The handover record should contain:

* Branch ID
* Business date
* Shift A ID
* Shift B ID
* Shift A closing employee
* Shift B receiving employee, where applicable
* Handover time
* Item quantities handed over
* Expected quantities
* Physical quantities
* Variances
* Comments
* Status
* Device or user responsible for submission

---

## 8. Accountant Review of Shift A

The accountant reviews Shift A independently after Shift B has opened.

The accountant may:

* Approve Shift A
* Request clarification
* Assign responsibility
* Create a staff recovery
* Create a team charge
* Write off an approved variance
* Reject an unsupported stock adjustment

Accountant approval must not interrupt active Shift B operations.

### Important correction rule

Once Shift B has opened, Shift A closing quantities must not be edited directly.

Any correction affecting stock must be posted as an auditable adjustment against Shift B or the currently open shift.

The adjustment must reference:

* Original Shift A
* Original item
* Original closing quantity
* Corrected quantity
* Difference
* Reason
* Accountant
* Date and time

This prevents the system from silently changing Shift B opening stock after operations have already started.

---

## 9. Reopening Shift A

Shift A may only be reopened when:

* Shift B has not yet been opened.
* The user has the required permission.
* A reopening reason is entered.
* The action is recorded in the audit log.

Once Shift B is open, Shift A must never be reopened.

Corrections must use adjustment transactions.

---

## 10. Shift B Closing

Shift B continues using:

`Shift A Physical Closing Stock`

as its opening stock.

Shift B then records its own:

* Issues
* POS consumption
* Other consumption
* Production
* Wastage
* Returns
* Transfers
* Closing physical count
* Variances

Shift B closing stock becomes the final kitchen closing stock for that business date.

That closing quantity becomes the next business date’s Shift A opening stock.

---

## 11. Required System Controls

The system must reject:

* Opening Shift B while Shift A is still open.
* Opening more than one Shift B for the same kitchen session.
* Posting Shift A transactions after handover.
* Editing Shift A closing quantities after Shift B has opened.
* Deducting Branch Main Store stock during the handover.
* Creating Shift B opening stock from expected stock instead of physical stock.
* Closing Shift A with incomplete item counts.
* Closing Shift A with unsubmitted wastage.
* Opening another kitchen shift while an applicable shift is already open.

---

## 12. Shift Status Flow

### Shift A

`OPEN`

→ `HANDOVER_IN_PROGRESS`

→ `CLOSED_PENDING_ACCOUNTANT_REVIEW`

→ `APPROVED`

or

→ `REVIEW_ACTION_REQUIRED`

### Shift B

`NOT_CREATED`

→ `OPEN`

→ `CLOSING_IN_PROGRESS`

→ `CLOSED_PENDING_ACCOUNTANT_REVIEW`

→ `APPROVED`

---

## 13. Mogogosiek Exception

This Shift A-to-Shift B logic applies only to branches configured as:

`TWO_SHIFT`

For the pilot:

* **Bomet Town:** `TWO_SHIFT`
* **Mogogosiek:** `SINGLE_SHIFT`

Mogogosiek must not create Shift B.

It has:

* One opening kitchen stocktake.
* One continuous kitchen shift.
* One final closing stocktake.
* No midday handover.

The most important rule is: **Shift B opens using Shift A’s physical closing count, not its expected closing stock, and the handover must not deduct stock from the Main Store.**
