## 🎯 What This Does

### In Simple Terms
- ✅ All HR adjustments are now automatically approved
- ✅ No waiting for manager approval
- ✅ Adjustments immediately included in payroll
- ✅ Works for both new and existing adjustments

### The Problem It Solves
**Before**: HR creates adjustment → Status: pending → Wait for approval → Finally in payroll
**After**: HR creates adjustment → Status: approved → Immediately in payroll ✅
